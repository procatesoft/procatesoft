# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security issue in any ProcateSoft project,
please report it privately. **Do not open a public issue.**

- Email: **info@procatesoft.com**
- Include: affected project, version/commit, reproduction steps, and impact.
- You will get an acknowledgement within 2 business days.

## Scope

This policy applies to repositories owned by **@procatesoft** and to
applications served under `procatesoft.com` and `erp.procatesoft.com`.

## Our commitment

- We follow OWASP guidance for secure design and code review.
- Confirmed issues are fixed before any public disclosure.
- Reporters are credited unless they prefer to stay anonymous.
