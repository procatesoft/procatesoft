# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it kindly.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, sustained disruption, and
publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **info@procatesoft.com**. Reports are reviewed promptly and
confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block
contributors who breach this policy.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant v2.1.
